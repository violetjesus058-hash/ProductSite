Kakobuy Spreadsheet: Catalog, Prices and Buying Agents

Kakobuy Spreadsheet is a free, browsable catalog of 1,148 Kakobuy and Taobao products, covering 14,535 style rows in six categories. Every row lists a product photo, product name, style variant, sizes, a USD display price and agent coverage. Displayed prices range from $1.07 to $192.65. The catalog was refreshed on October 29, 2026, and last fully price-checked on August 16, 2026. It is an independent resource maintained by Alex Finds and is not a store.

The catalog is a website, not a static Google Sheet. The browsing interface carries its own RIVORA Style Catalog header; RIVORA is the interface for this same catalog, not a separate collection. Readers can filter by category or buying agent, sort by price and search by product name without creating an account or downloading a file.

What each row contains

Each row represents one style variant, not one product. A row shows a reference photo from the listing, the product name, the style or colorway, the available size range, a USD display price, the category, and the number of platform links available for that listing. A single product can produce several rows, which is why 1,148 products expand to 14,535 rows.

What the catalog does not include

The catalog does not process orders, payments or shipping, and it does not calculate shipping cost. It does not store parcel weights, live stock levels, warehouse QC photos, ratings or seller reviews. Display photos are style references from listings, not QC photos of the item a buyer will receive.

How prices and dates work

Prices are converted to USD for display and refreshed in batches. The date on this page is the last refresh date, not a guarantee of live pricing. The last full price check was August 16, 2026. Catalog refresh dates and prices can change before checkout, so confirm the final price, line restrictions and shipping cost on the buying agent's own checkout page.

Beginner questions

Is the Kakobuy Spreadsheet free?

Yes. Browsing and searching the catalog are free, and no account is required. There is no paywall and no download step to view the table. You only pay a buying agent if you decide to place an order for an item.

Is it an official Kakobuy product?

No. It is an independent resource maintained by Alex Finds. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Kakobuy, and it does not process orders, payments or shipping.

How many products and style rows does it list?

As of October 29, 2026, the catalog lists 1,148 products and 14,535 style rows across six categories: Clothing, Accessories, Shoes, Pants, Bags and Watches. A style row is one variant of a product, such as one colorway or one size range.

Are the prices live?

No. Displayed USD prices are refreshed in batches rather than in real time. The last full price check was August 16, 2026, and the catalog was refreshed on October 29, 2026. Treat every price as a reference and confirm it at the agent's checkout.

Does the catalog show shipping cost or parcel weight?

No. It does not publish shipping rates and does not store measured weights. Shipping depends on the line, the destination and the packed weight or volume, so use the agent's shipping estimator or request a rehearsal before paying for international shipping.

What is a style row?

A style row is a single variant of a product, such as one colorway or size range. One product can have many style rows, so the catalog counts rows rather than products when it reports catalog size.

Can I download the catalog?

Yes. A CSV export of the full catalog is available as a direct download. The CSV mirrors the catalog columns and row count and can be opened in a spreadsheet for sorting and filtering.

Who maintains the catalog?

Alex Finds maintains the catalog as an independent project and has done so since October 29, 2025. Listings that fail review or can no longer be sourced are removed rather than left as stale rows.
